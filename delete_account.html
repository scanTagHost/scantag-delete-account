ScanTag Account Deletion Instructions

⚠️ Important: Deleting your account is permanent and irreversible. Once completed, you will not be able to recover your data.

How to Delete Your Account

  1. Open the ScanTag app and sign in to your account.
  2. Tap the menu button (☰) in the top left corner.
  3. Select "Delete Account" from the menu.
  4. You will see a warning explaining that all your data will be deleted. Tap Continue if you wish to proceed.
  5. On the final confirmation screen, you must press and hold the "Confirm Delete" button for 10 seconds to complete the deletion.

What Will Be Deleted

All of the following will be permanently deleted:
  • All your items / notes
  • All tags associated with your items
  • All generated QR codes
  • Your account information and profile
  • All cloud-synced data

Note: Any files you previously downloaded (such as QR code images or STL files) that are saved on your device will remain on your device.
